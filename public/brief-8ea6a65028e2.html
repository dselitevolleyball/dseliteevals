Template 1 · the priority

Game-day graphics — 12

Our players end up on opposite sides of the net when their schools meet. Each graphic celebrates one of those fixtures: two schools, the DS Elite players on each side, and the date.

What goes on each
  • Both school names, treated as a head-to-head.
  • The DS Elite player names on each side — up to 22 on one side, so the layout has to hold a long list without shrinking to nothing.
  • The date, and the level where one is given ("8th B", "Freshman"). Where no level is shown, every team at both schools plays that day.
  • A count is worth featuring on the big ones — "31 DS Elite players" carries the post on its own.

Template 2

Team cards — 20

One per club team, 215 players and 28 coaches in total. Squads run 4–13 players, so the template has to cope with both ends.

What goes on each
  • Team name (e.g. 14 Diamond), roster with jersey numbers, and the coaching staff.
  • Age group and level if it helps the hierarchy — the club runs Diamond, Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Topaz and Rise lines.
  • Optional second version per team carrying the tournament schedule — 27 tournament weekends across the club.
Template 3

School spotlights — 13

One per school where we have three or more players, showing who represents DS Elite there and which school team each made. Dripping Springs Middle alone has 22.

What goes on each
  • School name, our players grouped by their school team (7th A, 8th B, Freshman, Varsity…).
  • Each player's club team beside her name.
  • Schools with one or two players are in the data too, if you'd rather do a combined graphic than skip them.
