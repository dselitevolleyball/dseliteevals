DS Elite coaches · 2-minute walkthrough
Why it matters

Your time card is the app

Every hour you're paid for comes from one place: clocking in on DS Elite HQ.

💵 It's how you get paid

Hours are approved every Monday for the week before. Not clocked in means not on the sheet.

🏐 It's how we know the team was covered

The directors see who's in the gym. A missing clock-in looks like nobody was there.

⏱ It takes ten seconds

One tap when you walk in. Here's exactly how.

Step 1 · On the day

Tap “I'm here”

1
Open DS Elite HQ and go to Clock In.
2
Your shifts for today are listed under Your schedule today — team, time and hours. The next one is marked NEXT UP.
3
Tap I'm here when you arrive. It opens 30 minutes before the start, so you can do it walking in from the car.

Hours come from the shift, not the moment you tap. Two teams back to back show as one block — one tap covers both. Floating, subbing and S&A hours show up here too.

Step 2 · Forgot?

Log it the same week

1
On Clock In, scroll to Log a shift.
2
Pick the day you worked. Everything you were scheduled for that day is listed.
3
Tap the shift you worked. It's marked late and pays exactly the same.

Do it before Monday — that's when last week's hours are approved and sent to payroll.

Step 3 · Not on the schedule?

Set the hours yourself

Training, a coaches' meeting, covering a team last-minute, a practice that ran long:

1
Under Log a shift, pick what it was — Covering a team Training Floating …
2
Pick the team if there is one, then the start and end time. The hours count up as you pick.
3
Tap Log. It lands on your time card with everything else, and a director checks it before it's approved.
Step 4 · Can't make it?

Tell the app, not just Drew

📅 Planned — ask as early as you can

Home screen → 🗓 Request time off, then One practice off (we arrange cover) or Can't work a weekend (so you're not put on that tournament).

🤒 Same day — sick or an emergency

On Clock In, find the shift and tap Out next to Plan to. The directors get it straight away and start finding cover.

Always do it in the app, even if you also text. The app is what tells the sub, and it's what stops you showing up as a no-show on the time card.

Every Saturday

Your week, by text

Saturday morning you'll get a text and a notification with your week ahead:

✓
Every shift you're working, with the team and the time.
↔
Any practice you're covering, and who for.
✕
Anything you're not working because someone is covering you — and who.

Something wrong on it? Reply to that text. That's the fastest way to get it fixed before Sunday.

That's it

The short version

  • Tap I'm here when you arrive — it opens 30 minutes before.
  • Forgot? Log a shift before Monday. Late pays the same.
  • Not on the schedule? Log a shift and set the times.
  • Planned time off: Request time off, as early as you can.
  • Sick on the day: tap Out on the shift.
  • Check your Saturday text and reply if it's wrong.
