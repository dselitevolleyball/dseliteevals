Which logo to use

  • White or light backgrounds: the black “DS” version.
  • Black or dark backgrounds: the white “DS” version.
  • Over photos or on colored shirts: the all-white logo.
  • Square spaces (profile pictures, stickers, hats): the stacked diamond logo.
  • Printing anything? Send the printer a high-resolution PNG or the .ai vector file — they stay sharp at any size.

The ombré

The plum-to-pink fade is our signature.

  • It lives only on the letters ELITE, running from deep plum on the left to bright pink on the right.
  • Keep that direction — don’t flip it, and don’t add other colors to it.
  • DS is always solid — black or white, never pink or faded.
  • If your printer can’t do a fade (vinyl and some screen printing can’t), make ELITE solid DS Elite pink #C8216F, or print the whole logo in one color: all black or all white.

Please don’t

  • Stretch, squish or tilt the logo — resize it from a corner so it keeps its shape.
  • Change the colors, or add outlines, shadows or glitter effects to the logo itself.
  • Put the pink logo on a pink or busy background — use the all-white or black version instead.
  • Crowd it — leave some breathing room around the logo.

Selling something? If you’re making items to sell (fundraisers, team shops), please check with Coach Drew first.
