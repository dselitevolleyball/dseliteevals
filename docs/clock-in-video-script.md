# Clock-in video — script and shot list

**Length:** about 3 minutes. **Format:** screen recording of the app on a phone (Loom or iPhone screen record) with Drew talking over it. Record in portrait so it plays full-screen on a coach's phone.

**Where it goes:** SportsYou coaches group, the coach welcome email, and a "Watch how it works" link at the top of the Clock In screen (say the word and I'll add the link once the video has a URL).

---

## 0:00 — Why (15 seconds)

> Every hour you work gets paid from one place: the clock-in screen in DS Elite HQ. If it's not clocked in, nobody knows you were there, and it doesn't get paid on time. This takes ten seconds a shift. Here's exactly what to do.

**On screen:** the app home screen, then tap **Clock In & Timesheet**.

## 0:15 — On the day (40 seconds)

> Open the app when you get to the gym. Your shifts for today are listed under *Your schedule today*: the team, the time, and the hours. The one that's up next is marked NEXT UP.
>
> Tap **I'm here**. That's it. It opens thirty minutes before the practice starts, so you can do it walking in from the car. Hours are counted from the practice time, not from when you tapped.
>
> If you're on two teams back to back, it's one tap for both — they show as one block.

**On screen:** the schedule list, tap I'm here, the green "Checked in" state.

## 0:55 — Floating, subbing, S&A (25 seconds)

> Floating shifts and practices you're covering for someone show up here too, labelled. Same tap. A speed and agility hour with your team shows up next to the practice. Same tap.
>
> You'll never see two things for the same hour. If the schedule has you in two places at once, the Saturday text will tell you, so tell me.

**On screen:** a row with the FLOAT tag and a row with the SUB tag.

## 1:20 — Forgot to clock in (30 seconds)

> Forgot? Not a problem for two weeks. Scroll down to **Log a shift**, pick the day, and everything you were scheduled for that day is right there. Tap the one you worked. It's marked late, and it pays exactly the same.

**On screen:** the date picker, that day's shifts, tap one.

## 1:50 — Something that isn't on the schedule (30 seconds)

> Training, a coaches' meeting, covering a team last minute, a practice that ran an extra half hour: under **Log a shift**, pick what it was, the team if it applies, and set the start and end time yourself. The hours update as you go. Tap Log.
>
> This lands in the same place as everything else, and I see it before it's approved.

**On screen:** the role chips, the from/to time pickers, the hours count changing, tap Log.

## 2:20 — Calling out (30 seconds)

> If you can't make a shift: on the day's row there's **Plan to: Working / Out**. Tap **Out** and it tells the directors immediately so we can find cover. If it's further out, use **Request time off** and it goes on the calendar.
>
> Please do it in the app, not just a text to me. The app is what the sub sees, and it's what stops you being flagged as a no-show.

**On screen:** tap Out on a shift, then the time-off request.

## 2:50 — What you get back (15 seconds)

> Every Saturday you'll get a text and a notification with your week ahead: your shifts, anything you're covering for someone, and anything you're not working because someone is covering you. If it's wrong, reply to that text.
>
> Ten seconds a shift. Thanks, coaches.

**On screen:** the Saturday text on the phone.

---

## Expectations to say plainly somewhere in the video

- Clock in the day you work, from the gym. Not the next morning.
- Late is fine for two weeks. After that it may miss the pay run.
- Call out in the app, even if you also text Drew.
- Undo is next to every check-in if you tapped the wrong one.
- Hours are approved on Mondays; anything odd gets a question before it's paid.
